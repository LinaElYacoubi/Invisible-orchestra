Original sources identified by the collection:
- Piano: VSO2 / Versilian Studios (VSCO Community)
  http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html
- Acoustic guitar: University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples
  http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/
- Saxophone: Karoryfer Samples
  https://www.karoryfer.com/karoryfer-samples

Bundled MP3 files are unmodified copies of the selected upstream samples.
The app adjusts playback pitch and level, and crossfades a sustain loop for
saxophone in memory. No endorsement by the sample authors is implied.
See upstream-sample-source-info.txt and upstream-README.md in this folder.
upstream-LICENSE.md covers the upstream loader code; we do not use that loader.
